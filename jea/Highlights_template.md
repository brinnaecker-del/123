# Highlights {.unnumbered}

- Own revenue in Tibet's counties is far more unequal than spending or output.
- Transfers decouple county spending from local output in levels and changes.
- Equalisation of spending peaked in 2018–2022 and weakened after the pandemic.
- County revenue rises with output growth but does not fall in contractions.
- Output per capita shows β-convergence but no σ-convergence over 2016–2024.
